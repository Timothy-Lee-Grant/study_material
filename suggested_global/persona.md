# OUTLINE ONLY: `persona.md`

> **Not real content.** This is the proposed structure for `persona.md`. See `README.md` in this folder.

**Purpose:** who I am and where I'm going. The stable layer: it should still be true in three months.
**Target length:** under 150 lines (the current file is about 28 KB).
**Updated by:** the AI proposes a change when something major shifts (target role, job situation, a goal), and Timothy approves it. Never updated from a single session's evidence.

## Proposed sections

1. **In one paragraph.** Who I am, for a reader with thirty seconds.
2. **Background.** Electrical engineering; bare-metal embedded work; the six months on a .NET team. Described at the level of skills, with no employer or product names.
3. **Where I'm going.** The target role and why. The "rings" idea from the learning plan, in a few lines, with a link to the plan.
4. **Current situation.** What's true right now at a high level (for example: interviewing, which kinds of roles).
5. **How I want to be helped.** Candor; depth over brevity; tell me about gaps, including basic ones; tell me when to treat something as a black box. One line pointing to `learning_style.md` for everything about teaching.
6. **Constraints.** Time shape (short weekdays, walks, weekend blocks), tools and machines, standing choices (Python for coding problems, free resources).
7. **Lessons that changed how I work.** The short list of breakthroughs (interfaces as the API, inversion of control, domain model first), compressed to a line each.
8. **Changelog.**
